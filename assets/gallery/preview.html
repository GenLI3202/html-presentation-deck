Closing

Pick the calmest effect that still tells the story

  • Keep the screen still. Move only what changes.
  • Never zoom more than about 12% across the whole screen.
  • Do not go back and forth between an overview and its parts. Go back once, at the end.
  • Use motion on three to five slides of a talk, not on every slide.
  • Jumps (overview, links) and reduced-motion settings always switch at once.
Test

If someone looks away, they must still know where they are.
